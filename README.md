# Satyam Portfolio

Personal portfolio and creative technology studio site of **Satyam Kumar** — founder of Line Free India, builder of SatyamLabs, CodeAtlas and the AI Agents Lab.

**Live:** https://satyamkumar-builds.github.io/satyam-portfolio/

## What's inside

- 3D-style hero with a giant gradient wordmark and a real cut-out photo
- Five studio divisions as explorable portal cards — Video & Creative Studio, Web & Digital Experiences, AI Agents & Automation, Software & Product Development, Content & Digital Branding
- Live project showcases with real screenshots (Line Free India, AI Agents Lab, SatyamLabs, CodeAtlas)
- Eight embedded videos — editing samples, product films and the "20 Din, 20 AI Tools" avatar series
- Transparent starting-from pricing and an honest What I Do / Building / Exploring story section

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no framework, no tracking.
